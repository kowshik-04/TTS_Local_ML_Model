How this demo works

The browser streams 16 kHz PCM16 microphone audio to the agent server. Server-side VAD detects when you stop speaking, transcribes the turn, streams the LLM reply, and synthesizes each phrase with your cloned voice at 24 kHz. Starting to speak interrupts the current answer.

Conversation

Speak naturally; pauses end your turn. You can also type a test turn.
